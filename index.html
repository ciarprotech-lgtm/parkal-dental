From Humble Beginnings to Excellence

Parkal Dental Centre is a touchstone initiative in dentistry with over 13 years of clinical experience. Led by Dr. T. Saravanakumar, M.D.S, our clinic is committed to providing affordable and quality oral care in the most pleasing and hygienic environment possible.

With top-notch equipment and techniques, we ensure every visit is friendly and productive. We promote long-term patient relationships through education and informed consent, making sure you understand every step of your treatment.

Every patient who walks through our doors receives the same attention and care that built our reputation — comprehensive treatment plans, gentle procedures, and a genuine commitment to your oral health and beautiful smile.
