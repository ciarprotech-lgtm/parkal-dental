Our Treatments & Services

Comprehensive dental care with modern technology and personalized treatment plans designed to give you the healthy, beautiful smile you deserve.

Teeth Whitening

Professional bleaching procedures to safely and dramatically brighten your teeth by several shades.

Bonding

Composite resin matching your tooth color to quickly repair minor chips, cracks, or gaps.

Check-Ups

Comprehensive diagnostics and examinations to catch issues early and maintain oral wellness.

Cosmetic Procedures

Tailored aesthetic enhancements to design and construct your dream smile line.

Dental Implants

Permanent screw-retained restorations replacing missing teeth from root to crown.

Dentures & Bridges

High-grade partial or complete prosthetics to restore masticatory function and speech.

Emergency Care

Urgent dental interventions for severe trauma, acute pain, bleeding, or dislodged restorations.

Extractions

Gentle removal of severely compromised, crowded, or unsalvageable teeth to preserve health.

Fillings & Sealants

Composite fillings to restore decayed areas and sealants to shield vulnerable grooves.

Laser Dentistry

Minimally invasive therapies utilizing modern lasers for precise gum surgery and disinfection.

Mouth Guards

Custom-fitted protective guards for sports and night guards to prevent teeth grinding (bruxism).

Oral Surgery

Complex surgical procedures involving hard and soft tissues of the mouth, jaws, and face.

Paediatrics

Specialized, friendly pediatric care focusing on dental development in infants and pre-teens.

Root Canals

Meticulous cleaning of infected pulp pathways to save natural teeth and relieve severe pain.

Teeth Cleaning

Professional scaling and polishing to remove stubborn plaque, tartar, and surface stains.

Teeth Reshaping

Subtle enamel recontouring to optimize the length, contour, and alignment of individual teeth.

Veneers & Crowns

Thin porcelain shells and custom caps to cover imperfections and fortify damaged teeth.

X-Ray

Advanced low-radiation digital imaging to inspect bones, roots, and hidden dental structures.

INVISALIGN

Premium clear aligner therapy using SmartTrack material to straighten teeth virtually invisibly.

Facial & Jaw Pain Therapies

Specialized clinical therapy for TMJ dysfunctions, teeth clenching, and muscular jaw pain.

Braces (Metal, Ceramic, Self-Ligating)

Comprehensive orthodontic options including low-friction self-ligating brackets and ceramic braces.

Aligners

Custom thermoplastic trays designed to sequentially shift your teeth into alignment.

Wisdom Teeth Removal

Expert extraction of impacted or poorly aligned third molars to prevent crowding and infection.

Root Canal Treatment

Advanced endodontic therapy utilizing micro-instrumentation for long-term clinical success.

Implants

Titanium post placements to establish a solid foundation for crowns, bridges, and dentures.

Child Dentistry

Gentle dental checkups and cavity prevention in a fun, fear-free environment for young kids.

Ceramic Zirconia Crowns & Bridges

Premium, biocompatible metal-free zirconia restorations offering unmatched strength and aesthetics.

Need a specific treatment not listed? Contact us for personalized care.
